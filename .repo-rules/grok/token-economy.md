# Token economy (Grok bots)

**Updated:** 2026-10-16 (Pier-approved)

Grok tokens are spent when a bot is woken. Keep wakes small.

1. **1:1 DM.** Each task goes by DM to the **one** bot that must act (wake chain: [wake-rules.md](./wake-rules.md)).
2. **Groups are the exception.** Product Council and project groups are for **fleet-wide news or rule changes only** — never daily tasks. A group post wakes **every** member.
3. **New conversation per task.** Same bot, new chat. Do not delete or duplicate the bot. Canonical: [wake-rules.md](./wake-rules.md) (Grok chats) and [../common/models-and-usage.md](../common/models-and-usage.md). Wrap-up in `docs/STATUS.md`.
4. **No 2-bot groups.** Two bots talk in 1:1 DM, not a room of two.

Rooms and who sits where: [channels.md](./channels.md).
